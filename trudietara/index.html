My Story

From Corporate to Creative Life

Years working in London and corporate America left me feeling unfulfilled, uninspired and empty. I felt stuck and unable to spread my wings in a world that didn't see me or allow me space to express my soul gifts.

That desire sparked a big change and led me to a path that's given my family freedom, inspiration and a new timeline. Now I help women create real time financial freedom and live a life they love - without sacrificing what matters most to them.
